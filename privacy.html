Privacy notice

Google Sign-In and Supabase Auth handle student sign-in. The site stores session information in your browser to keep you signed in and saves preferences such as theme locally.

Information you share

Supabase stores account profiles, study activity, enquiries and uploaded files. Contributed notes can become available to other students immediately, together with the contributor name and optional social links you supply. Only upload documents you intend to share.

Corrections and deletion

You can remove your contributed notes from your account. For account access, corrections, deletion or a question about retention, contact CA Junction.
