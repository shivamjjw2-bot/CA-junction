Privacy notice

Setup draft: Before accepting real student accounts, the site owner must replace this notice with their verified business contact details and confirm the practices below.

CA Junction is designed to collect account details, CA level, study plans, test activity, service enquiries, note submissions and payment references needed to provide its services. Account and uploaded content is stored using the connected Supabase project. Student note PDFs are reviewed by an administrator before publication.

Payments

UPI payment access is unlocked only after an administrator verifies the transaction against the receiving account. Do not upload payment screenshots or share passwords, OTPs, or bank PINs.

Your choices

For account access, correction or deletion requests, contact the CA Junction owner through the WhatsApp contact linked on the home page once it is configured. The owner should add a monitored email or other contact method here before launch.

Owner details

Owner/business name and privacy contact: to be supplied by the CA Junction owner.
